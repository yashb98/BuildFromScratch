## What Qwen3-0.6B-Base was actually trained on

Per the Qwen3 tech report (verbatim summary):

- **Corpus:** 36T tokens across 119 languages
- **Mix:** web + PDF-extracted (Qwen2.5-VL OCR pipeline) + synthetic STEM/code (generated by Qwen2.5-Math and Qwen2.5-Coder) + books + reasoning data
- **Three-stage pretraining:**
  - Stage 1: 30T tokens, **4K context length**, broad coverage
  - Stage 2: +5T tokens, increased STEM/code/reasoning ratio, still 4K context
  - Stage 3: long-context extension to **32K**, high-quality long-context data
- **Recipe (paper baseline experiment):**
  - Optimizer: **AdamW, eps=1e-8** (betas not surfaced in summary; standard Qwen3 LLM β = 0.9, 0.95)
  - Schedule: **cosine**, linear warmup 0 → **1.7e-3** over **1,000 steps**, cosine decay to **3.2e-4** over **500B tokens**
  - Batch size: **constant 4M tokens** (baseline), or dynamic **2M → 4M → 5M → 6M** at 125B-token intervals
  - Sequence length: **32K** (final stage); 4K for stages 1 & 2
- **Caveat:** the specific 0.6B-Base recipe is NOT fully disclosed. The 1.7e-3 / 1000 / 500B baseline is the paper's experimental anchor; the production 0.6B-Base may differ. We treat this as the best available faithful target.

**Bottom line for reproduction:** 36T tokens on one GB10 is infeasible. Even Stage-1's 30T tokens × 4M-token batch = 7.5M steps is multi-decade compute. The faithful build will match the **per-step hyperparameters** (LR, schedule shape, optimizer, betas, eps) and use a **dramatically reduced token budget** with a public corpus proxy.

## Recipe candidates

Each recipe applies to the from-scratch model produced in Phase 4. AdamW(0.9, 0.95) + weight_decay 0.01 + grad_clip 1.0 are constant across all three (these are Qwen3 paper defaults that also match the existing SmolLM2 `train.py`).

| Recipe | LR (peak) | LR (end) | Schedule | Warmup | Token budget | Seq len | Batch (tokens) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Faithful-scaled (Recommended)** | **1.7e-3** | 3.2e-4 (cosine) | cosine | 1,000 steps | 1B–4B (scaled by user budget) | 2048 | ~262K (128 × 2048) | Qwen3 paper §recipe-baseline, scaled |
| B — SmolLM2 baseline (existing `train.py`) | 3.0e-3 | 0 | WSD | 200 steps | 1B–4B | 2048 | ~262K | Mirror of `SmolLM2-134(base)/train.py` |
| C — Modern alternative (WSD + D2Z) | 1.7e-3 | 0 (decay-to-zero) | WSD, 20% decay phase | 200 steps | 1B–4B | 2048 | ~262K | [Through the River, Schedule-Free / D2Z, 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.09846); WSD outperforms cosine in same-compute settings |

Notes:
- **Recipe A** is the most faithful: same LR shape as the paper. The token budget is the scaling lever — paper used 500B, we use 1–4B (~250–1000× smaller). Will dramatically underfit Qwen3-Base; eval signal is PPL improvement vs random-init, not closing the gap to Qwen3-Base on real benchmarks.
- **Recipe B** is what's already in this codebase; chosen for the SmolLM2 reproduction. Slightly more aggressive peak LR (3e-3 vs 1.7e-3) and the WSD schedule shape. Useful primarily as a comparison anchor.
- **Recipe C** combines Qwen3's peak LR with WSD D2Z — emerging consensus (per 2026 papers) is that WSD + decay-to-zero beats cosine at matched compute. This is **not faithful to Qwen3 specifically** but is the modern recommendation. I'd reserve this for the MODERNIZED build, not faithful.

## Chinchilla sanity check

- Chinchilla-optimal: ~20 tokens/param → 596M × 20 = **~12B tokens**.
- Recipe A at 1B tokens = ~1.7 tokens/param → **~12× under-trained vs Chinchilla** (flagged per skill rule).
- Recipe A at 4B tokens = ~6.7 tokens/param → ~3× under-trained.
- This is fine for a reproduction exercise (we're not chasing SOTA), but the eval verdict must say "scaled-down by ~12×; expect large PPL gap to released Qwen3-0.6B-Base."

## Recommendation

- **Data: FineWeb-Edu (sample-10BT)** — closest public analogue of Qwen3's knowledge-dense pretraining mix; HF's standard 10B-token sample fits cleanly on local disk; clean enough that 1–4B tokens go far per-step.
- **Recipe: A (Faithful-scaled)** — matches paper's LR shape (cosine, peak 1.7e-3, end 3.2e-4). Diverges from paper only in the token budget, which is unavoidable.

## Open questions

1. **Compute budget**: how many wall-clock hours are you willing to spend on the faithful train? (1B tokens ≈ 30–60 min on a GB10 at bf16; 4B tokens ≈ 2–4 hr; needed to lock the token budget for Phase 8.)
2. **Sequence length**: skill default is 2048; Qwen3 paper used 4K in stage 1. Mirror the paper (4K, slower per step) or stay at 2048 (faster, less position-coverage)?
3. **Smoke run first?** I'd recommend a 1000-step smoke train on TinyStories (mirrors `train_tinystories.py`) before the full faithful train, just to validate the bf16 stack on this new model. Adds ~10 min. Recommended.
